Walkie is an ideation app: place PDFs, photos, files, shapes and notes on a board over 10 m × 10 m and edit them together while on a group call and chat — on the same screen. The app is available in English, Korean and French.

What you can do

🗺 Everything on one huge board

PDFs you can flip and search, photos, files, video, YouTube, pens, shapes, connectors, sticky notes, tables, mind maps and charts on one canvas.

🎙 Call and chat without leaving

One button starts a group call next to the board; chat, replies and reactions in the same window. Recording only in rooms where the host allows it, always shown.

👀 Live cursors and laser

See teammates' pointers, point with the laser (L) so everyone sees a glowing trail, react with emoji or raise your hand.

⏱ Timer and dot voting

Show one timer to everyone and vote on ideas with dots; keep the result on the board.

💬 Comments and @mentions

Comment on any object and @mention members to notify them. Send photos and files in chat.

📊 Excel and PowerPoint

Paste an Excel range or insert a workbook (up to 2,000 × 50) with its charts; link it and the board updates when you save. Export the board as editable .pptx.

🧩 Frames, templates, versions

8 templates (meeting notes, brainstorm, retro, kanban, novel plot, case analysis, lean canvas, study). Frames export as PDF pages or slides; save versions and restore later.

🔒 Your content stays on devices

Chats, boards and files are not kept on servers. Invited members' devices exchange signed, encrypted updates directly.

Provided as an APK (not on Google Play yet). Scan the QR code with your phone.
